# Great Valley: a showcase of map events. Format: scripts/event_script.gd
# (and docs/events.md).

== start ==
@narrate The Great Valley, at dawn.
@camera (26, 1)
General: They'll come over the bridges. Let them come.
@camera (2, 18)
Lord: Three bridges, one river. We cross before nightfall.
Ember: There's another dragon on their side. Let me talk to it before anyone swings an axe.
Lord: Then stay close to it. We'll keep the rest busy.

== turn 2, enemy ==
Messenger: General! They're already at the river!
General: Hold the fort. The valley does the rest.

== talk Ember, Wyrm ==
Ember: You don't have to fight for them.
Wyrm: They pay well.
Ember: We'll pay better. Fly with us.
Wyrm: ...Fine. Point me at the General.
@recruit Wyrm
@flag wyrm_joined

== battle General ==
General: You crossed my valley just to die in it?

== battle General | if wyrm_joined ==
General: Even the dragon turned on me?

== death Ember ==
Ember: Sorry... I couldn't...
