== start ==
@narrate The royal palace, well past midnight.
Rosalind: Ten days until the ceremony. Ten days, and then a lifetime of ribbons and bridges.
Rosalind: Unless... someone simply left. Tonight. To see the sea.
Rosalind: The watch sleeps at its posts. If I don't linger near them, they'll never know.
@camera (13, 6)
@narrate Meanwhile, in the palace kitchen...
Doran: Three months' pay, and the steward says "come back next season". Fine. We'll take it in hams.
Bex: Captain! Someone else is robbing the kitchen!
Fennick: Burglars. Ours is the honest kind of theft, at least.
Mags: Less talking. More hitting.
@camera (2, 2)

== talk Rosalind, Doran ==
Rosalind: Excuse me! Are you burglars? Real ones?
Doran: We're the Copper Kettle Company, and we're collecting what we're owed. Who's asking?
Rosalind: Rose. A, um, commoner. Of the common sort. I need an escort to the coast, and I can pay. Later.
Fennick: (Commoner. In a silk nightgown. With a tiara in her pocket.)
Doran: "Later" is how the palace pays, too. ...Fine. Anything beats hams. Company, we're leaving.
@recruit Doran
@recruit Bex
@recruit Fennick
@recruit Mags
@flag joined
